# OTB USA optimization profile

Use this profile when `/evolve optimize site:<area>` targets the OTB USA landing.

## Priority order

1. **Offer clarity** — visitor understands what OTB USA is in seconds.
2. **Legal safety** — Boston/Harvard claims stay descriptive and no-affiliation compliant.
3. **CTA quality** — WhatsApp intent is specific, concise and routed through the SSOT.
4. **Conversion UX** — mobile-first hierarchy, clear objections, minimal friction.
5. **SEO/GEO** — page explains MBA, Business Aesthetic Health, Boston/EUA and Grupo US consistently.
6. **Performance** — static Astro, low JS, image priority, LCP/CLS/INP gates.

## Experiment output shape

Use:

```xml
<answer>
  <baseline>What exists now and why it may underperform.</baseline>
  <experiment>Smallest safe OTB-specific change.</experiment>
  <validation>How to verify behavior, copy, legal and build.</validation>
  <log_entry>Durable learning if the experiment succeeds.</log_entry>
</answer>
```

## Negative constraints

- Do not import other product journeys or CTAs.
- Do not make unsupported Harvard affiliation claims.
- Do not add JS unless interaction requires it.
- Do not change price/date/location without source confirmation.
